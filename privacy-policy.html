Privacy Policy

Rail Rota Companion · Effective Date: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rail Rota Companion (“the App”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and being transparent about what data we handle.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects and processes the following categories of information:

  • Sync Code: A unique code you enter (or scan via QR code) to link the App to an existing Rail Rota account. This code is used solely to retrieve your roster data.
  • Driver Display Name & Station: Your display name and home station, as set in the main Rail Rota app, are retrieved to personalise your experience.
  • Roster & Duty Data: Your shift schedule, duty cards, and any manual overrides are downloaded from the cloud to display your calendar.

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Contact lists, call logs, or SMS messages
  • Photographs or media (the camera is used exclusively to scan QR codes and no images are stored)
  • Financial or payment information
  • Health or biometric data
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data

2. How We Use Your Information

All data retrieved by the App is used exclusively to:

  • Display your duty roster and shift calendar
  • Show duty details including sign-on/sign-off times, locations, and movements
  • Cache your schedule locally on your device so you can view it offline

We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, analytics, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to displaying your roster.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Cloud Storage: Your roster data is stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore. Data is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Local Storage: A cached copy of your roster is stored on your device using encrypted local storage so you can access your schedule offline.
  • No Additional Servers: The App does not operate its own backend servers. All cloud data is managed through Google Firebase infrastructure, which complies with industry-standard security practices.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties. Your roster data is never disclosed to advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

The only third-party service used is:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): Used to store and sync your roster data. Firebase is operated by Google and subject to the Firebase Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Camera Permission

The App requests access to your device camera solely to scan QR codes for entering your sync code. No photographs are taken, stored, or transmitted. You may deny camera access and manually enter your sync code instead.

6. Data Retention

Your cached roster data is retained on your device until you:

  • Remove the driver profile from the App
  • Clear the App’s data through your device settings
  • Uninstall the App

Cloud-stored roster data is managed by the primary Rail Rota app. Deleting the Companion app does not affect your cloud data.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored about you (visible directly within the App)
  • Delete your local data at any time by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling it
  • Withdraw camera permissions at any time through your device settings

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
