1 Introduction

This privacy policy applies to both Rail Rota (the "Main App") and Rail Rota Companion (the "Companion App"), collectively referred to as "the Apps" or "our Services". The Apps are developed and maintained by an independent developer ("we", "us", or "our").

Rail Rota is a personal roster management app designed for London Underground train drivers to view and manage their duty schedules.

Rail Rota Companion is a read-only companion app that allows a driver's partner, family, or friends to follow the driver's live working schedule.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

2 Data We Collect

No account or login is required to use either app. We do not collect email addresses, passwords, or government-issued identification.

2.3 Automatically Generated Data Both Apps

5 Companion App Pairing & Data Sharing

When a Rail Rota user shares their Sync Code (via QR code or text), they are granting the recipient read-only access to the following information:

  • Display name and tube line
  • Duty schedule (sign-on/sign-off times and locations)
  • Daily notes and duty overrides (swaps, annual leave, rest days)
  • Train movement details
Important: Sharing your Sync Code is entirely voluntary. You can revoke access at any time by generating a new Sync Code from the Backup & Restore screen, which will immediately disconnect all linked Companion apps.

The Companion App is strictly read-only. Companion users cannot edit, delete, or modify any of your data.

6 Data Storage & Security

Local Storage

The majority of your data is stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage (encrypted at rest by your device's OS). This includes your imported duty books, E.I.R.F. records, notepad content, tracked trains, and app preferences.

Cloud Storage

When you use Cloud Backup or the Companion App sync feature, a subset of your data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. This data is:

  • Encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Identified only by your anonymous Sync Code — not your real name or email
  • Stored in Google Cloud data centres subject to Google's security practices

7 Data Retention

  • Local data is retained until you uninstall the app or manually clear it.
  • Cloud backup data is retained until you overwrite it with a new backup or delete it by request.
  • Swap Board posts are automatically removed after their relevant date has passed.

8 Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including UK GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access — View all data stored about you (most is visible directly in the app).
  • Rectification — Edit your display name, phone number, and other profile data at any time.
  • Erasure — Delete your cloud backup by uninstalling the app, or contact us to request cloud data removal.
  • Portability — Export a full backup of your data as a JSON file from Utilities → Backup & Restore.
  • Withdraw Consent — Disable personalised ads in your device settings, or stop using cloud sync at any time.

9 Children's Privacy

The Apps are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
